# M00 — Product Strategy and Requirements

## Status

**Approved — M00 strategy baseline complete.** This records agreement on product direction and scope; it does not claim that application functionality has been implemented.

## Strategic purpose

Define the product's identity, target users, value, boundaries, initial release, and requirements before detailed UX or implementation.

## Approved product definition

The Website Design Builder is a user-directed, game-like website wireframing experience. Users create their own website design by independently choosing high-level components, patterns, configuration options, and order. Levels and missions teach and guide the process; they do not make design decisions for the user.

The initial release is not an AI designer. It has no AI-generated content or design, no AI recommendations, and no automatic component selection.

## Decisions baseline

1. **Audience:** broad audience, initially optimized for small businesses and independent professionals.
2. **User control:** users choose patterns, customize available options, and freely arrange/reorder components.
3. **Validation:** only objective required-mission completion is checked. No subjective design-quality score.
4. **Content timing:** users may provide content upfront or progressively.
5. **Progression:** users can move backward and forward freely and revise any decision.
6. **Game mechanics:** levels and mission completion only. No points, badges, prizes, achievements, leaderboards, timers, or reward economy.
7. **Initial output:** complete visual website wireframe/design, not a production website.
8. **Responsive design:** curated responsive patterns and guided user choices.
9. **AI:** excluded from the initial release; optional future capabilities may be considered through a separate decision.
10. **Assets:** wireframe placeholders are the default. Users may optionally provide photos, logos, or reference imagery; final images are not required.
11. **Partial completion:** an incomplete project remains useful and communicates the user's purpose, decisions, current design, placeholders, and remaining work to a developer or later implementation workflow.

- Content and image-placeholder behavior is clear.
- Levels, free navigation, completion, and absence of rewards are unambiguous.
- Complete and partial project outcomes are defined.
