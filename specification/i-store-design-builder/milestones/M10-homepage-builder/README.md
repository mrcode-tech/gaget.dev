# M10 — Homepage and Section Library

## Strategic purpose

Enable the player to build a useful homepage aligned with their project's goals.

## Objective

Provide a contextual library and guided composition flow for essential homepage regions.

## Scope

- Header/navigation
- hero
- main offering
- benefits
- gallery/listing
- trust
- FAQ
- contact/conversion
- footer
- section variants
- contextual relevance
- structured content slots.

## Out of scope

Full page-template marketplace and complex specialized business workflows.

## Dependencies

M06–M09.

## Acceptance criteria

A player can compose a coherent homepage from curated sections; irrelevant section types are not forced; the primary visitor action is clear.

## Deliverables

Complete homepage builder journey.
