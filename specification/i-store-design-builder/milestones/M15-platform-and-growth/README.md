# M15 — Platform Readiness and Growth

## Strategic purpose
Make the experience reliable for ongoing use and prepare expansion based on validated demand.

## Scope
- Authentication and account lifecycle.
- Project dashboard and cloud persistence.
- Recovery, version history, and project duplication.
- Sharing and collaboration foundations.
- Template/starter-project library.
- Product analytics and feature flags.
- Admin/support capabilities.
- Security, privacy, backup, monitoring, and operational readiness.
- Subscription and payment capabilities only when business validation supports them.

## Out of scope
Unvalidated monetization or marketplace features and operational complexity without demonstrated need.

## Dependencies
M05–M14, delivered in staged releases.

## Acceptance criteria
Account and project operations meet defined security, privacy, reliability, and recovery expectations without compromising the core design experience.

## Deliverables
Platform operations, account/project lifecycle, and approved growth capabilities.

## Definition of done
- [ ] Threat/privacy and data lifecycle reviews completed.
- [ ] Backup, restore, and recovery tested.
- [ ] Monitoring and support runbooks documented.
- [ ] Usage analytics respect privacy requirements.
- [ ] Feature rollout and rollback procedures exist.
- [ ] Operational acceptance criteria pass.

**Status:** Planning.
