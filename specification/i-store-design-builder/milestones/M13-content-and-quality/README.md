# M13 — Content, Media, and Website Quality

## Strategic purpose
Make the design credible, complete, accessible, and ready for handoff.

## Scope
- Structured content editor and field types.
- Image upload, asset library, crop, and focal-point controls.
- Asset reuse, alt text, and content placeholders.
- Missing-content and missing-asset detection.
- Navigation, internal-link, and structural validation.
- Design consistency and responsive checks.
- Accessibility fundamentals: contrast, keyboard, focus, semantics, labels, and alternative text.
- Actionable review and readiness summary.

## Out of scope
Full CMS operations, advanced SEO suite, analytics-driven optimization, and automated rewriting without user approval.

## Dependencies
M09 identity; M10 homepage; M11 responsive; M12 multi-page.

## Acceptance criteria
The system identifies missing or invalid content and structural issues, explains their impact, and suggests actionable fixes without silently changing the player's choices.

## Deliverables
Content/media workflow, validation rules, quality review, and readiness report.

## Definition of done
- [ ] Content and asset schemas documented.
- [ ] Upload and asset lifecycle tested.
- [ ] Validation rules and severity levels defined.
- [ ] Accessibility and responsive checks verified.
- [ ] Issues link to relevant correction actions.
- [ ] Acceptance and regression tests pass.

**Status:** Planning.
