# Product Vision

## Vision

Enable people without professional design skills to create a coherent, purposeful, multi-page website design by completing a guided, game-like journey.

## The problem

Many individuals and organizations know what they offer but do not know how to translate that knowledge into website structure, visual identity, layouts, and responsive behavior. Traditional design tools expose too many low-level decisions; conventional website builders can still require users to understand templates, components, and design terminology.

## The proposed experience

The player describes their project, receives contextual guidance, and completes a sequence of missions. Each mission introduces a manageable design decision. The website canvas remains visible from the beginning and progressively becomes a real preview of the player's work.

## Target audiences

- Small-business owners and entrepreneurs
- Independent professionals and service providers
- Creators, artists, and portfolio owners
- Community groups and non-profit organizations
- Organizations and teams needing an informational website
- People exploring a website idea before commissioning implementation

## Value proposition

- Turn an intimidating task into manageable decisions.
- Explain design concepts in plain language.
- Recommend patterns appropriate to the player's purpose.
- Show the impact of decisions immediately on the canvas.
- Produce a reusable design blueprint for implementation.

## Product principles

1. **Purpose before appearance:** understand the audience and desired outcome.
2. **Guided freedom:** curated options, not an unrestricted editor.
3. **Visible progress:** the website canvas is present from the start.
4. **Progressive disclosure:** reveal complexity only when useful.
5. **Reversible decisions:** allow revision without punishing experimentation.
6. **Coherence over novelty:** encourage consistency and usability.
7. **Industry neutrality:** adapt to user context rather than assuming a store or other specific business.
8. **Handoff by design:** preserve structured decisions, not just screenshots.

## Non-goals for the core experience

- Replacing professional vector/UI design tools.
- Infinite canvases or arbitrary pixel placement.
- Requiring users to write CSS or understand layout mathematics.
- A full general-purpose CMS or e-commerce backend in the first release.
- Competitive leaderboards, timers, or punitive scoring.
- Generating production code before the design model is stable.
